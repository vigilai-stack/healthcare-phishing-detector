Check a clinic email before you click

Paste the message or upload a .eml file. The check runs on this computer. It does not open links, and it does not replace your clinic's security process.

The two examples are fictional and were held out of training. Files are limited to 1 MB and are not saved.

How to read the score

0–29 Low. No strong warning sign stood out.

30–59 Needs a closer look. Check with a phone number you already have.

60–79 Suspicious. Do not click, open, or reply.

80–100 High risk. Treat it as phishing and tell IT.

A familiar topic such as a claim or a lab result is not, by itself, a high score. A look-alike web address, a mismatched reply address, or a risky attachment can raise the score even when the wording is calm.
